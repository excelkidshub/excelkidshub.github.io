Our Phonics Programs

Structured levels for every child — from first letter sounds to fluent, independent reading.

Level 1 – First Steps to Reading

Children learn all 42 letter sounds, how to blend sounds into words, and build early reading confidence. Perfect starting point for children aged 4–6.

Level 2 – Growing Readers

Children advance to two-letter sounds (like "ch", "sh", "ai"), tricky words, and start reading simple sentences with growing fluency. For children who have completed Level 1.
