Why Online Phonics Classes Work

Many parents wonder whether online phonics is as effective as in-person learning. The answer — when delivered correctly — is yes. Our live, interactive sessions are not pre-recorded videos. Your child attends a real class with a real teacher and real classmates.

  • Live sessions: Real-time teaching, correction, and encouragement — not just videos to watch
  • Small batches: Maximum 8–10 children so every child is seen and heard
  • Interactive tools: Digital whiteboards, phonics activities, and sound exercises in every session
  • Progress tracking: Regular updates to parents on what your child has covered and achieved
  • Flexible timing: Morning and evening slots to fit your family routine

What Your Child Gains

  • Strong decoding skills: Read new words independently without guessing
  • Accurate spelling: Understand the sound-letter connections behind every word
  • Reading fluency: Progress from sounding out letters to reading sentences smoothly
  • Confidence: Success in reading builds confidence across all school subjects
  • Consistent practice: Access to our digital reading studio between sessions

Frequently Asked Questions

What platform do you use for online classes?

We use Google Meet or Zoom. Both are free, work on any device, and require no technical setup beyond a device and internet connection.

Can children outside India join?

Yes. We have students from the UAE, UK, Australia, and the US. Time zones may affect batch availability — contact us and we will find a slot that works.

What equipment does my child need?

A tablet, laptop, or computer with a camera and microphone. A mobile phone works in a pinch but a larger screen is better for activities. Stable internet connection (4 Mbps or above) is recommended.

Is online phonics as effective as offline?

For structured phonics instruction delivered live — yes. Our students show the same progress rates online as in our Dhanori centre. The key is live teaching, small groups, and consistent attendance.

How many sessions per week?

3 sessions per week (each 60 minutes) for best results. Each batch runs for 20–35 sessions depending on the level.
