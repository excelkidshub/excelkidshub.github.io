What is Jolly Phonics?

Jolly Phonics is a world-renowned, research-based phonics program used in over 100 countries. It teaches children the 42 letter sounds of English using a multi-sensory approach — combining actions, songs, and visual cues to make learning engaging and effective.

Unlike traditional rote memorisation, Jolly Phonics gives children the tools to decode any new word independently. It is widely used in UK schools and has strong evidence for improving reading and spelling outcomes in children as young as 3–4 years.

  • Multi-sensory learning: Each sound has an action, a story, and a song
  • Systematic progression: 42 sounds taught in a specific, proven order
  • Proven results: Used in schools across UK, India, and 100+ countries
  • Child-friendly: Designed for ages 3–10 — fun, not stressful

The 5 Jolly Phonics Skills Your Child Will Learn

1. Learning Letter Sounds

All 42 phonemes of English, taught using actions and mnemonics. Children learn to hear and produce each sound accurately.

2. Learning Letter Formation

Correct pencil grip and letter formation from the start — building handwriting alongside reading skills.

3. Blending

Children learn to blend individual sounds into words — the core skill that unlocks reading independence.

4. Identifying Sounds in Words

Segmenting spoken words into their component sounds — the foundation of accurate spelling.

5. Tricky Words

Common words that do not follow phonics rules are taught separately so children can read fluently from the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jolly Phonics suitable for Indian children?

Yes. Jolly Phonics teaches the sounds of English which are universal. It is widely used in Indian international schools and is highly effective for children learning English as their primary or second language.

What age should my child start Jolly Phonics?

Most children start between ages 4–6, but older children who missed structured phonics can start at any age. We assess each child and recommend the right entry point.

Do you use original Jolly Phonics materials?

Yes, we use authentic Jolly Phonics workbooks and resources. Parents may be asked to purchase the pupil workbook — we will guide you on what is needed.

Can I join online from outside Pune?
