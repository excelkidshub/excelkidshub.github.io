What is Jolly Phonics? A Parent's Complete Guide

Everything you need to know about the world's most popular phonics program — explained simply for parents.

If you have been exploring phonics options for your child, you have almost certainly come across the name "Jolly Phonics." It appears on school websites, tutors' profiles, and parent groups — but what exactly is it, and why does it work so well?

1. What is Jolly Phonics?

Jolly Phonics is a structured, multi-sensory phonics program developed in the UK by Sue Lloyd and Sara Wernham in the 1990s. It is now used in over 100 countries and is the most widely used phonics program in the world.

The program teaches children the 42 letter sounds of English — not just the 26 alphabet letters — using a combination of actions, songs, and visual mnemonics. Each sound has its own action that helps children remember it physically, not just mentally.

2. Why 42 Sounds and Not 26 Letters?

English has 26 letters but 42 sounds (phonemes). For example, the letter "a" makes different sounds in "cat," "cake," and "call." The combination "ch" makes a single sound not covered by either "c" or "h" alone.

Jolly Phonics teaches all 42 sounds, not just letter names. This is why children taught with Jolly Phonics can read words they have never seen before — they have the tools to decode, not just recognise.

3. The 5 Skills of Jolly Phonics

The Jolly Phonics program develops five core literacy skills:

  • Learning the letter sounds: All 42 phonemes, taught in a specific order designed to get children reading real words as quickly as possible
  • Learning letter formation: Correct pencil grip and letter writing — developed alongside reading, not separately
  • Blending: Combining individual sounds to read words — the core skill that unlocks reading independence
  • Identifying sounds in words (segmenting): Breaking spoken words into their component sounds — the foundation of accurate spelling
  • Tricky words: Common words that do not follow phonics rules (like "the," "said," "was") are taught separately so children can read real texts from early on

4. How Does the Multi-Sensory Approach Work?

The most distinctive feature of Jolly Phonics is its multi-sensory approach. Every sound is taught with:

  • An action: For the "s" sound, children weave their hand like a snake and say "sssss." For "a," they pretend to be an ant biting their arm. These actions create a physical memory anchor for each sound.
  • A song or chant: Each sound group has a song that makes the sounds memorable even when children are not in class
  • A visual cue: Each letter comes with an illustration connected to the sound (e.g., a snake for "s")

This multi-sensory approach is particularly effective for young children, who learn through doing and experiencing rather than passive listening.

5. What Order Are the Sounds Taught?

Jolly Phonics teaches sounds in a specific order designed to get children reading real words as quickly as possible. The first group — s, a, t, i, p, n — allows children to build dozens of three-letter words (sat, pin, tap, nip) almost immediately. This early success builds enormous confidence.

The order is not alphabetical — it is pedagogically sequenced for maximum early reading impact.

6. At What Age Should Children Start Jolly Phonics?

Jolly Phonics is designed for children aged 3 to 6 as their introduction to reading. However, older children who missed structured phonics can also benefit greatly — especially children in Class 1–4 who are struggling with reading or spelling.

There is no upper age limit for learning phonics. The approach adapts — older learners cover the same content at a faster pace without the songs and actions used for very young children.

7. Is Jolly Phonics Used in Indian Schools?

Yes — Jolly Phonics is increasingly used in Indian international schools, CBSE schools, and private academies. Many schools adopt it for Nursery and KG classes. Private phonics academies like ExcelKidsHub use it as the foundation for all programs from Level 1 through Reading Excellence.

8. How is Jolly Phonics Different from Regular School Phonics?

Many Indian schools teach phonics in some form, but the approach is often inconsistent, rushed, or mixed with whole-word memorisation. Jolly Phonics is a complete, systematic curriculum — every sound is taught, in the right order, with the right multi-sensory tools. Children who learn Jolly Phonics from a certified trainer develop significantly stronger reading and spelling foundations than those who receive ad hoc phonics instruction.
