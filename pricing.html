Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Digital Reading Platform? +

Our Digital Reading Platform is an online learning system where children can access interactive phonics books, audio support, and practice activities. It's designed to supplement classroom learning and build reading confidence through self-paced practice.

Does this include live classes? +

No, these subscriptions are for the Digital Reading Platform only. They do not include live online or offline phonics classes. Live teacher-led classes are offered separately through ExcelKidsHub Phonics Academy.

Can my child use it on mobile? +

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Your child can learn anywhere with an internet connection.

Is internet required? +

Yes, an internet connection is required to access the Digital Reading Platform. However, some content can be downloaded for offline use depending on your subscription plan.

Can I upgrade later? +

Yes, you can upgrade your subscription at any time. Simply contact our support team or choose a higher plan from your dashboard. The upgrade cost will be prorated.

How long do I get access? +

Subscriptions are valid for 30 days from the date of activation. You can renew at any time. Your child's reading progress is always saved — even after a subscription expires, progress is preserved and resumes exactly where you left off when you renew.

Can multiple children use one account? +

Each subscription is intended for single-child use to ensure accurate progress tracking. For multiple children, we recommend separate accounts or contacting us for family plans.
