Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Digital Reading Platform?+

An online system where your child can access interactive phonics books, audio support, and practice activities — designed to build reading confidence through self-paced practice.

Does this include live phonics classes?+

No. These subscriptions are for the Digital Reading Platform only. Live teacher-led classes are offered separately through ExcelKidsHub Phonics Academy.

Can my child use it on mobile?+

Yes. The platform works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Your child can learn anywhere with an internet connection.

Is internet required?+

Yes, an active internet connection is required to use the Digital Reading Platform.

How long does the subscription last?+

Paid subscriptions are valid for 30 days from activation. Your child's progress is always saved — even if the subscription expires, progress resumes from where you left off when you renew.

Can I upgrade later?+

Yes. You can switch to a higher plan at any time from the pricing page. Contact support if you need assistance.

Can multiple children use one account?+

Each subscription is for one child to ensure accurate progress tracking. For multiple children we recommend separate accounts.
