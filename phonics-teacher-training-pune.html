Who Is This Training For?

School Teachers

Primary and pre-primary teachers who want to teach phonics confidently using the Jolly Phonics method in their classrooms.

Private Tutors

Tutors who work with children one-on-one or in small groups and want to offer structured phonics as a core service.

Parents

Parents who want to support their child's reading journey at home using the same proven techniques used in ExcelKidsHub classes.

Aspiring Teachers

Individuals who want to start a career teaching phonics — at home, online, or in schools across India.

What You Will Learn

  • Jolly Phonics methodology: The complete 42-sound system, teaching order, and learning objectives
  • Multi-sensory techniques: Actions, songs, visual cues — how to make phonics engaging for children
  • Classroom management: How to run structured phonics sessions for groups of 5–15 children
  • Assessment: How to identify where a child is on their phonics journey and what to teach next
  • Blending and segmenting: Teaching children to decode and encode words independently
  • Tricky words: How to teach high-frequency words that don't follow phonics rules
  • Resources and materials: What you need to set up your own phonics classes
  • Online teaching: How to deliver Jolly Phonics effectively in a live online format

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a teaching background to join?

No. The training is open to teachers, tutors, and parents. A love of children and a commitment to learning is all that is required. Swati will guide you through everything from basics to classroom application.

Is this a Jolly Phonics official certification?

The training follows the Jolly Phonics methodology and is led by a UK-certified Jolly Phonics trainer. Participants receive an ExcelKidsHub Certificate of Completion. For the official Jolly Learning certification, additional steps through Jolly Learning UK may be required.

Can I attend online from outside Pune?

Yes. Most of our teacher training batches are online (Google Meet). You can join from anywhere in India.

What materials do I receive?

Trainees receive session notes, a list of recommended resources, and guidance on setting up their own phonics practice. Jolly Phonics workbooks (if required) can be purchased separately.

How many seats are available per batch?

Maximum 12 participants — to ensure every trainee gets sufficient practice time and personalised feedback.
