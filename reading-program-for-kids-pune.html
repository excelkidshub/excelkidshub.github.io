What is a Structured Reading Program?

A structured reading program is not just "extra reading practice." It is a systematic, step-by-step curriculum that teaches children how reading actually works — starting from individual sounds, building to words, then sentences, then full texts.

At ExcelKidsHub, our reading program is built on the Jolly Phonics method — the world's most widely used structured literacy approach. Children do not just memorise words; they understand the system behind them, which means they can read any word they encounter.

  • Phonics-based: Teaches the 42 sounds of English systematically
  • Multi-sensory: Actions, songs, writing, and reading — all in every lesson
  • Graduated: Each level builds on the previous — no gaps, no guessing
  • Expert-led: UK-certified trainer with 10+ years teaching children in Pune

Who Benefits Most from Our Reading Program?

Early Starters (Ages 4–6)

What Makes ExcelKidsHub Different from Tuitions

Many parents try general English tuition when their child struggles to read. Tuition typically focuses on textbook exercises and exam preparation — it does not address the root cause of reading difficulty.

ExcelKidsHub targets the foundation: phonemic awareness and decoding skills. Once a child can decode, every other reading skill improves naturally. Our students consistently move from struggling readers to independent readers within 2–4 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

My child is in Class 4 — is it too late to start a reading program?

Not at all. Children of any age benefit from structured phonics. Older children with reading gaps catch up quickly once the foundational sounds are in place. We assess every child before placing them in a batch.

How is this different from school reading classes?

School classes cover many subjects with large groups. Our reading program is dedicated, small-batch, and specifically structured for phonics and reading fluency — with individual attention in every session.

How long until my child shows improvement?

Most children show noticeable improvement within 6–8 weeks. Full reading independence typically develops within 4–6 months of Level 1.

Is there a digital reading component?

Yes. Subscribers get access to our digital reading studio with interactive phonics workbooks, games, and practice exercises — great for daily practice at home between classes.
